# RENDER BEAST — Video Workflow
# CINEMA BEAST Step-by-Step Cinematic Production Pipeline

---

## OVERVIEW

CINEMA BEAST produces complete video pre-production packages, shot-by-shot generation prompts, and post-production instructions. Every video is designed as a coherent narrative sequence — not a collection of visually impressive clips.

---

## STEP 1 — BRIEF INTAKE → CINEMA BEAST

**Reference**: `workflows/17-intake-and-brief.md`

Classify the project:

| Type | Duration | Complexity | Deliverable |
|---|---|---|---|
| Short social clip | 5-30s | Low | Scene breakdown + clip prompts |
| Product commercial | 15-60s | Medium | Full pre-production package |
| Brand film | 1-5min | High | Complete production package |
| Cinematic narrative | 5min+ | Very High | Full production bible |

Extract at minimum:
- Video type and purpose
- Duration
- Platform(s)
- Emotional journey (what feelings do we want to create?)
- Key message
- Brand/creator identity
- Available assets (voiceover? existing footage? music?)

---

## STEP 2 — CREATIVE CONCEPT DEVELOPMENT

**Reference**: `references/10-video-production.md` (Pre-Production Framework)

Develop the central creative concept:
- **The concept sentence**: What is this video about? (Not what happens — what it means)
- **The narrative structure**: Three-act, commercial, social, or other
- **The emotional progression**: How does the viewer feel at each beat?
- **The visual world**: What does the environment and atmosphere look like?

**Originality check** (`references/13-anti-slop-quality-gates.md`):
- Does this concept rely on cinema slop patterns? (generic cinematic grade, unmotivated slow motion, meaningless drone pull-out)
- Is there at least one creative decision that makes this specific to this brief?

**Output**: Creative concept document

---

## STEP 5 — SCENE BREAKDOWN (TIMELINE ENGINE)

**Reference**: `references/10-video-production.md` (Timeline Engine)

Break the video into individual scenes. Use the full Scene Specification Format for every scene.

**Scene count guidance**:
- 15-second spot: 3-5 scenes
- 30-second spot: 5-8 scenes
- 60-second spot: 8-15 scenes
- 2-minute film: 15-25 scenes
- 5-minute film: 25-40 scenes

**Anti-slop check for each scene**:
- Does every scene advance the narrative or emotional arc?
- Is every camera movement motivated?
- Is there an edit motivation (emotion, action, sound)?

**Output**: Complete scene-by-scene breakdown

---

## STEP 6 — SHOT LIST

Convert the scene breakdown into a production shot list:

```
SHOT LIST
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
Shot | Scene | Size | Angle | Movement | Duration | Description | Priority
S01  | SC01  | EWS  | EL    | Static   | 3s       | [Desc]     | A
S02  | SC01  | CU   | HA    | Push-in  | 5s       | [Desc]     | A
S03  | SC02  | MCU  | EL    | Handheld | 4s       | [Desc]     | A
S04  | SC02  | ECU  | GL    | Static   | 3s       | [Desc]     | B
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
Priority: A = Required / B = Preferred / C = Optional if time
Abbreviations:
  Sizes: ECU/CU/MCU/MS/MW/WS/EWS
  Angles: EL=Eye Level, LA=Low Angle, HA=High Angle, BEV=Bird's Eye, OV=Overhead
  Movements: Stat=Static, PIn=Push In, POut=Pull Out, PL=Pan Left, PR=Pan Right, 
             TU=Tilt Up, TD=Tilt Down, TL=Track Left, TR=Track Right, HH=Handheld
```

**Output**: Numbered shot list

---

## STEP 7 — CINEMATOGRAPHY PLAN

**Reference**: `references/10-video-production.md` (Cinematography System)

For the entire video, establish:
- **Lens vocabulary**: Which focal lengths are used and when
- **Movement vocabulary**: Which camera movements are used and when
- **Consistency rules**: What stays the same throughout
- **Variation strategy**: What changes and why (e.g., handheld during action, static during intimate moments)
- **Coverage strategy**: Which scenes need A-camera + B-camera

**Output**: Cinematography plan document

## STEP 12 — GENERATION EXECUTION

If connected to video generation tool:
1. Submit shots in order
2. Review each clip for continuity compliance
3. Flag continuity breaks before proceeding
4. Generate bridge shots if transitions require them

If no connected tool:
- Deliver complete pre-production package labeled "PRODUCTION SPECIFICATION — requires generation tools"
- Include recommended generator for each shot type
- Include assembly instructions

---

## STEP 13 — CLIP INSPECTION

For each generated clip, inspect:
- Subject accuracy vs. specification
- Camera movement correctness
- Lighting consistency with continuity spec
- Color grade consistency
- Technical quality (resolution, artifacts)
- Duration accuracy

**Anti-slop check**:
- Is there an unmotivated slow motion applied?
- Is there a heavy color grade applied to flat footage?
- Is there any AI continuity break (character appearance changed)?

**Decision**: Accept / Adjust prompt and regenerate / Flag for post-production fix

---

## STEP 14 — ANTI-SLOP GATE

**Reference**: `references/13-anti-slop-quality-gates.md`

Apply cinema-specific slop detection:
- Generic cinematic grade without concept
- Unmotivated slow motion
- Meaningless drone footage
- Generic corporate B-roll patterns
- Fake continuity between shots
- Camera movement without purpose

**Output**: Anti-slop report and resolution

---

## STEP 15 — QUALITY SCORING

**Reference**: `evals/22-quality-evaluation.md`

Score the complete video package (or specification if clips not generated):
- Video-specific evaluation criteria apply (see `22-quality-evaluation.md`)
- Score each scene and provide overall average
- Flag any scene that falls below 75
